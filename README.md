# 🌅 Sunset Grocery & Blessings

**Sunset Grocery & Blessings** is a full-stack grocery store management platform that combines efficient retail operations with a warm, sunset-inspired user experience. Designed for modern grocery businesses, the system streamlines inventory management, sales tracking, financial record-keeping, and partner account management while fostering a positive work environment through a daily blessings and inspiration portal.

Built with **React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Firebase**, the application delivers a secure, responsive, and scalable solution for store owners and staff.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔐 Secure Multi-Role Authentication

* Multi-role access control with protected administrative areas
* Secure 4-digit PIN lock system for sensitive operations
* Google Authentication and email/password sign-in
* Account recovery, re-authentication, and secure account switching

### 📦 Smart Inventory Management

* Real-time inventory tracking and stock monitoring
* Barcode scanning through device camera integration
* Product image capture and visual inventory logging
* Category management and low-stock alerts

### 🛒 Sales & Billing System

* Integrated Point of Sale (POS) interface
* Digital receipt generation and transaction processing
* Sales history and transaction logs
* Tax calculation and basket management
* Voice-assisted inventory search and transaction entry

### 📊 Analytics & Business Insights

* Sales reporting and performance tracking
* Inventory status dashboards
* Business metrics for informed decision-making

### 🤝 Partner Ledger Management

* Supplier and customer account management
* Credit and balance tracking
* Outstanding payment monitoring
* Historical ledger records and audit trails

### 🌸 Daily Blessings & Inspiration Portal

* Daily motivational messages and affirmations
* Positive business reminders and spiritual reflections
* A unique feature designed to promote productivity and well-being

## 🏗️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, Vite
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion
* **Backend & Database:** Firebase Firestore
* **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication & Google OAuth
* **Features:** Camera Integration, Barcode Scanning, Voice Input

## 🎯 Project Goal

To provide grocery businesses with a modern, secure, and user-friendly management system that simplifies daily operations while creating a positive and inspiring working environment.
